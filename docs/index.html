Penname

Everyone answers. Nobody signs. Guess who said it.

Penname is a daily party game for group chats. Each day your group gets one question. Everyone answers anonymously, then you match each answer to the friend who wrote it.

Play Penname, free

Features

  • One link for your chat: friends join with a nickname. No app, no account, no email.
  • A new question every day at local midnight, the same for every group.
  • Answers stay anonymous until everyone has guessed.
  • Points for spotting friends and for fooling them, a weekly table and streaks.
  • Works on any phone or computer, including inside WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.
  • No ads, no tracking.
