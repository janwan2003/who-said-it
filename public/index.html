How it works

1

Share one link

Create a group and paste the link in your chat. Friends open it, pick a nickname and they're in. No downloads.

2

Answer today's question

Everyone gets the same question each day and answers in secret. Nobody sees the answers until everyone has written one.

3

Guess who said what

Match each answer to its author. When everyone has guessed, the names are revealed, with points, a weekly table and streaks.

Questions

Does everyone need to install something?

No. Penname runs in the browser on any phone or computer. Opening the link in WhatsApp, Messenger, iMessage, Slack or Discord is enough.

How many people can play?

From 3 to 30 people per group. It works best with 4 to 10.

What if someone doesn't answer?

Guessing starts by itself when everyone has answered. If someone is slow, anyone who has answered can start the guessing once 3 answers are in. Latecomers sit that round out.

Is it really anonymous?

Until the reveal, nobody can see who wrote what, including the person who made the group. After everyone has guessed, the names are shown to the group. That's the game.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
