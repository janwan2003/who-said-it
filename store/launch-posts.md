## 2. Product Hunt (weekday, 00:01 PT)
- **Name:** Penname
- **Tagline (≤60):** The daily "who said it?" game for your group chat
- **Description:** One question a day for your friends, family or team. Everyone answers anonymously, then you match
  each answer to the person who wrote it. Names are revealed once everyone has guessed, with points, a weekly table
  and streaks. Works from one link in any browser (also inside WhatsApp/Messenger), no app, no account, free.
- **First comment:** I wanted the "guess who sent it" game people play by hand in group chats, without one person
  having to collect answers. Penname does the collecting: one link, everyone answers in secret, guessing starts
  when all are in. Feedback on the questions especially welcome.
- **Topics:** Games, Social, Web App. **Gallery:** `public/og.png` + phone screenshots from `tests/e2e/run.mjs`
  (`shots/answered-phone.png`, `guess-phone.png`, `reveal-phone.png`).
