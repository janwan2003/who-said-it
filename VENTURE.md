## Thesis
Daily group game: each day one prompt goes to the group, everyone answers anonymously, and once all have answered you guess who wrote what. Scores per group, streaks.

## Market evidence
Same daily-group shape as the operator's Plain (playplain.app), which grows from friends; a companion with a different mechanic, not a copy.
(Seeded by the operator session on 2026-11-20; verify with web search and add links before building.)

## Our edge
K above 1: needs the whole group to answer; reveal when all answered; screenshot-worthy answers; groups spawn sub-groups (family, work, friends). Daily ritual.
Loop: needs others. No login. 30-second join from a chat link on a phone.
